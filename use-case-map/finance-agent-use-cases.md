# AI Agents in Finance: 10 Potential Use Cases 

1. Financial Planning & Analysis: Explain budget-versus-actual variances.
2. Financial reporting: Extract and organize KPI information.
3. Valuation: Review assumptions and organize DCF inputs.
4. Investment Research: Retrieve cited information from company filings.
5. Credit: Review borrower information and covenant requirements.
6. Treasury: Summarize cash, liquidity, and interest-rate exposure.
7. Compliance: Organize regulatory documents, identify required actions and notify user of new regulations when released.
8. Audit: Assist with evidence organization and preliminary compliance scanning.
9. Client work: Prep meeting briefs, proposal drafts and presentation decks.
10. Analyst productivity: Organize research, notes, tasks and follow-ups.

# Mock Use Case for an Agentic System in Finance

## Monthly FP&A Review System
Finance manager asks:
"Explain why Op Income missed budget and what we need to do for next month to hit budget"<br>
<div align="center">  
Agentic System to solve this problem<br>
↓<br> 
1. Data Agent<br>
Pulls approved budget, actuals, KPI, and prior-period data.<br>
↓<br>
2. Variance Agent<br>
Calculates and explains revenue, expense, margin, and operating-income variances.<br>
↓<br>
3. Driver Agent<br>
Identifies likely business drivers: volume, price, labor, marketing, inventory, or one-time costs.<br>
↓<br>
4. Forecast Agent<br>
Updates next-month forecast assumptions and creates scenarios.<br>
↓<br>
5. Validation Agent<br>
Checks calculations, missing data, unusual results, and unsupported claims.<br>
↓<br>
6. Report Agent<br>
Creates a structured management summary with charts, findings, risks, questions, and recommended follow-up actions. <br>
↓<br>
7. Human Finance Manager<br>
Reviews, corrects assumptions, approves the final report.<br>

</div>
